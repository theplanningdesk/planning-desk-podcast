Privacy policy

The Planning Desk is a weekly podcast and video about supply chain planning and design. This page covers the podcast website, the RSS feed and the "Planning Desk uploader" app that publishes episodes to the show's own YouTube channel.

What we collect

Nothing from listeners or viewers. The website and feed have no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and podcast apps, YouTube and GitHub may log requests under their own privacy policies.

The uploader app

The uploader uses Google's YouTube Data API only to upload episodes, captions and thumbnails to The Planning Desk's own channel. It does not access, store or share any other Google account data, and it is not offered to other users. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Content

Episodes are produced with AI: AI agents gather and summarise news from public sources, and the hosts' voices are synthetic.
