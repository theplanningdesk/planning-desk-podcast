The Planning Desk

Supply chain planning and design news with a focus on the Nordics and the DACH region: a quick briefing every weekday morning, a book worth your time every Saturday and a fifteen-minute week in review every Sunday. Disruptions, trade and macro signals, planning technology and AI, real company use cases, the events worth your time and the books behind good planning.

The Planning Desk is produced with AI. AI agents gather and summarise news and books from public sources, and the hosts' voices are synthetic.
